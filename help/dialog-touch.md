# Touch

Actions ▸ **Touch…** in the [Folder Compare view](topic:view-folder-compare) sets the modification time of the selected
items:

- **Copy timestamps from other side**, to the Left or the Right: each item takes the time of its counterpart.
- **Set to** a date and time, `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS` in your local time (also `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM`, `YYYY-MM-DD`, or an RFC
  3339 time with its offset). It starts as now, or as the last time set. **Touch on**: Left, Right or Both.

A text that is not a time shows the message in place of the preview and Start stays off. The preview and **Start** work
as in [Copy to Folder](topic:dialog-copy-to-folder). See [hikasa folder op](topic:folder-op) `touch --time`.
