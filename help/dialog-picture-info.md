# Picture Compare Info

Session ▸ **Picture Compare Info** (Ctrl+I) shows the statistics of a [Picture Compare view](topic:view-picture-compare):

- **Pixels**: the positions covered by a picture, how many are same, similar and different (with their percentage),
  the positions only one side covers, the largest and the mean distance, and the bounds of the differences.
- **Geometry**: the size of the compared area, the offset, the orientations, Auto Scale and the side it scaled.
- **Options**: the tolerance, the mode, Ignore Unimportant Differences and the verdict; then any warnings.

**Copy** places the same lines `hikasa picture compare` prints on the clipboard. **Show** zooms the view onto the
bounds of the differences.
