# The Home view

The Home view is the start of every new tab. Type or paste two paths and press Enter or Compare: two folders
open a [Folder Compare view](topic:view-folder-compare), two files a
[Text Compare view](topic:view-text-compare). The comparison replaces the Home view in its tab.

A path that does not exist, or a folder paired with a file, is reported under the fields. See
[The main window](topic:main-window).
